Glossary & how this dashboard reads the data

Not ready = at hub or supplier, greenlight requested or pending departure (status 02, 04, 07). In transit = shipped (08). Delivered = arrived or received (09, 10).
Weekly chart groups delivered value by the ISO week of arrival (ATA). Volume is taken once per shipment from Shipping_Info.
PTAEO = Project, Task, Award, Expenditure type, Organisation: the funding codes shown under Funding in each request.
Request = Order Request (OR) + Destination Country. References containing SR (NBO_SR, DKR_SR, SR_…_hub) are stock releases pushed by HQ from hubs; OR_… and other country references are order requests raised by the country. Both count toward the country total.
Item status: Pending = not available at hub, in procurement or awaiting greenlight · Processing = picked & packed or pending departure · In transit · Delivered = arrived or received.
Shipment = WHO Shipping Reference (NBO_SR, DKR_SR, 26GVA, SHP-). Weight, volume and temperature from Shipping_Info.
Fulfilled from = HUB/Supplier + SHP Source: Stock-AF-NBO → Nairobi, Stock-AF-DKR → Dakar, Stock-HQ-DXB → Dubai, Stock-HQ-GVA → Geneva; Supply Type = Procurement → supplier PO.
Status = WHO Shipping Status: 02 at hub/supplier · 04 greenlight requested · 07 pending departure · 08 shipped · 09 arrived · 10 received · 11 canceled.
Needs attention: greenlight pending over 7 days, picked at hub over 14 days without dispatch, supplier readiness date passed, or ETA passed while still in transit.
Value = PO Line Amount (USD). Delivered % = arrived + received value over non-canceled value.
