How each tab reads the OSL export and how the estimates are calculated.

Statuses are shown exactly as written in the export, including its spelling (Awating, MIssing). The Procurement tab lists all 17; a status with no lines shows where those lines are in the export, usually under another emergency tag.

Dates

Request Date date the order request or stock release was raised.
PO creation / stock request date the supplier PO or hub stock request was created.
Committed readiness date the supplier or hub committed the goods would be ready (PO/OPR Committed readiness date).
Last readiness latest recorded readiness date (Last Readiness Date).
ETD Estimated Time of Departure from the hub or supplier.
ETA Estimated Time of Arrival in country.
ATA Actual Time of Arrival. The export holds ETA and ATA in one column; it is read as ATA when the status is 09 or 10, otherwise as ETA.

Requests, shipments and sources

Request = Order Request (OR) + Destination Country.
Order request (OR) raised by the country office (OR_…, country references).
Stock release (SR) pushed by WHO from hub stock (NBO_SR, DKR_SR, SR_…_hub). Both count toward the country total.
Shipment = WHO Shipping Reference. Weight, volume and temperature come from Shipping_Info.
Fulfilled from = HUB/Supplier: Stock-AF-NBO Nairobi, Stock-AF-DKR Dakar, Stock-HQ-DXB Dubai, Stock-HQ-GVA Geneva, Stock-DRC-KIN Kinshasa; Supply Type Procurement = supplier PO.
Supply Type Stock = WHO hub stock · Stock (free) = free-of-charge stock · Procurement = bought on a PO · Inkind = donated by a partner · 3rd Party = supplied by a third party · Unspecified = empty.
Origin PO / OPR ref purchase order number and the OPR or PO reference on the request.
PTAEO Project, Task, Award, Expenditure type, Organisation: the funding codes under Funding.

Calculations

Value = PO Line Amount (USD). Canceled lines are excluded from value and line counts.
Delivered status 09 or 10, including from transit hub. Not yet delivered = every other status except 11.
Delivered % shown as lines delivered ÷ lines ordered and as delivered value ÷ ordered value. The export has no partial-delivery quantity, so a line is delivered in full or not.
Request → arrival median days from Request Date to ATA, over delivered lines with both dates.
Requested vs delivered line value or count by week of Request Date against week of ATA; cumulative mode shows the gap.
Needs attention greenlight pending over 7 days, picked at hub over 14 days without dispatch, supplier readiness passed, or ETA passed while in transit.

Beneficiaries & risk

Consumption rates come from the Ebola treatment centre supply calculator export (100,000 beds, 3 months). Each item’s need is divided back to a rate per bed per month, e.g. 170 coveralls, 1,530 exam gloves and 212.5 medical masks per bed-month.
ETC runway is the number of days the stock lasts at the bed count shown, assuming full occupancy. It is limited by the scarcest of coveralls, exam gloves and FFP2 respirators. Lots are used in arrival order and stop counting at their expiry date.
Patients and lives bed-days ÷ average stay gives patients treated. Lives saved = patients × (fatality without care − fatality with care). Defaults: 48.2%, WHO’s crude fatality for the current Bundibugyo outbreak in DRC (DON, 6 Oct 2026), and 30%, the low end of the 30–50% WHO reports for past Bundibugyo outbreaks. WHO publishes no fatality rate for treated patients, so this is an estimate, not a WHO figure.
WHO standard kits use the beneficiary numbers WHO publishes per module (2025 kit overview, TESK 2025 note, RH kit manual). The primary care population counts IEHK 2024 basic modules only (1,000 people each, 3 months). Supplementary units are hospital-level for the same population and are listed separately, not added (the OSL Kits Impact dashboard adds the two). Kit rates and the arrived (status 09/10) vs pipeline split follow the OSL dashboard. One filovirus RT-PCR kit = 96 reactions; patient tests exclude the controls on every run (set controls per run in Tweaks).
Pipeline lines use their ETA, or a forecast from today: greenlight +14 days, at hub +7, departed or in transit +3. Hub impact splits each country’s patients by which hub supplied the limiting item.
Coverage Comms, cold chain, tents and visibility items are enablers and are not counted. Items are matched by WHO code. Expiry dates are only available for Nairobi stock releases. Populations are approximate UN 2024 estimates. The tab shows the value linked to an impact rate for the current selection.

Data source

OSL export data (3).xlsx: 14,185 lines across 51 destination countries and every emergency tag. The Procurement tab reads all of it by default (Emergency = All emergency tags, all countries). The other tabs default to 2026-G013 (3,380 lines) and the priority countries set in Tweaks.
Emergency filter (top bar) = Emergencies/Activities. “All Ebola / Marburg / VHF tags” adds Ebola DRC, Ebola UGA 25, Ebola preparedness, Filovirus prepositioning, Marburg and Suspect VHF. “All emergency tags” includes every line. Beneficiaries & risk always uses 2026-G013.
Country names are taken as recorded. “Tanzania, United Republic Of” is merged with Tanzania and “Ivory Coast” with Côte d’Ivoire. “Congo” (54 lines) is kept separate because the export does not say which Congo. 2 lines with “Destination Country” in the country column are shown as Country not recorded. 3 footer rows (Total, No filters applied, blank) are not data and are skipped.
Shipping_Info weight, volume and temperature exist for 112 of 372 shipment references.
